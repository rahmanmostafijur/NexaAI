# Customer Membership Policy

## Nexa Rewards Overview

Nexa Rewards is the free loyalty programme of Nexa Commerce Ltd. Every registered customer is automatically enrolled. Customers are placed in one of four tiers based on their total spend over the **last 12 months** (rolling), excluding cancelled orders and refunded amounts.

## Membership Tiers

| Tier | Rolling 12-month spend |
|---|---|
| Bronze | ৳0 to ৳24,999 |
| Silver | ৳25,000 to ৳74,999 |
| Gold | ৳75,000 to ৳199,999 |
| Platinum | ৳200,000 or more |

Tiers are reviewed on the **first day of every month**. A customer is upgraded as soon as they cross a threshold, and is downgraded only if their rolling spend stays below their tier's threshold at the monthly review.

## Nexa Points

- **100 points = ৳50** discount, redeemable at checkout in multiples of 100 points.
- Points can cover up to 30% of an order's value.
- Points expire **12 months** after they are earned.
- Points are deducted if the order they were earned on is returned or refunded.

## Terms and Conditions

- Tier discounts cannot be combined with clearance or "Final Sale" prices, but they can be combined with Nexa Points.
- Tier discounts do not apply to gift cards or to the Nexa Extended Warranty Plan.
- Corporate and bulk orders (20 or more units of one product) are excluded from tier discounts and are priced by the corporate sales team.
- Nexa may suspend membership benefits in cases of fraud or abuse of the return process.
- Nexa may change these benefits with 30 days' notice published on the website.
