# Employee Handbook

## Welcome to Nexa

This handbook explains the policies, benefits and expectations that apply to all permanent employees of Nexa Commerce Ltd. It does not replace your employment contract or Bangladesh labour law; where they differ, the contract and the law take priority. The People Operations team reviews the handbook every year.

## Working Hours

The standard work week is **Sunday to Thursday**. Office hours are **9:00 AM to 6:00 PM** with a one-hour lunch break. Friday and Saturday are the weekly holidays.

Employees may use **flexible hours**, starting between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM, provided they are available during the core hours of **10:00 AM to 4:00 PM** and complete 8 working hours per day. Warehouse and customer support teams work on rosters published two weeks in advance.

## Leave Policy

**Annual leave:** Employees receive **20 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave starts accruing after the **3-month probation period**. Up to **10 unused days** can be carried over to the next year; any remaining days lapse. Annual leave requests should be submitted in the HR portal at least 7 days in advance.

**Sick leave:** **14 days** of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than **2 consecutive days**.

**Casual leave:** **10 days** per year for personal matters, taken with at least one day's notice where possible. Casual leave cannot be carried over.

**Maternity leave:** **16 weeks** of fully paid maternity leave (8 weeks before and 8 weeks after childbirth), available to employees with at least 6 months of service.

**Paternity leave:** **10 working days** of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 3 months of the birth or adoption.

**Bereavement leave:** **3 days** of paid leave on the death of an immediate family member.

**Public holidays:** Nexa observes the public holidays in the government calendar. Employees who work on a public holiday receive a substitute day off within 30 days.

Unpaid leave of up to 30 days may be granted by the department head for exceptional circumstances.

## Remote Work

After completing probation, employees in eligible roles may work remotely up to **2 days per week** with their manager's approval. Remote workers must:

- be reachable on company chat during core hours;
- use only company-issued laptops and the company VPN;
- never access customer data from public Wi-Fi without the VPN.

Nexa provides a monthly internet allowance of **৳1,500** to employees on an approved hybrid schedule.

## Equipment and Information Security

Company laptops, phones and access cards remain Nexa property and must be returned on the last working day. Passwords must never be shared. Customer personal data may only be accessed for a legitimate business need, and data incidents must be reported to security@nexashop.example within 24 hours.

## Code of Conduct

Employees are expected to act with integrity, respect and professionalism. Nexa has **zero tolerance** for harassment, discrimination and bullying. Concerns can be raised with a manager, People Operations, or anonymously through the ethics hotline.

**Conflicts of interest:** Employees must declare any financial interest in a supplier, competitor or customer.

**Gifts:** Employees may accept gifts from business partners worth up to **৳2,000**. Gifts above this value must be declared to People Operations and are usually declined.

**Dress code:** Business casual in the office. Customer-facing store and warehouse staff wear the Nexa uniform provided by the company.

## Compensation and Benefits

**Salary:** Salaries are paid by bank transfer by the **5th working day** of each month.

**Festival bonus:** Employees receive **two festival bonuses** per year, for Eid-ul-Fitr and Eid-ul-Adha, each equal to **50% of the monthly basic salary**. Employees in their probation period receive a pro-rated bonus.

**Performance reviews:** Reviews happen twice a year, in **June and December**. Salary increments are effective from January.

**Health insurance:** Group health insurance covers the employee, spouse and up to two children, with inpatient coverage of up to **৳300,000** per year.

**Learning budget:** Each employee has a learning budget of **৳30,000 per year** for courses, certifications and books, approved by their manager.

**Expense reimbursement:** Work-related expenses are reimbursed within **30 days** of submitting a claim with original receipts in the HR portal.

**Provident fund:** Employees contribute 10% of basic salary and Nexa matches it after one year of service.

## Resignation and Notice Period

The notice period is **30 days** for employees and **60 days** for managers and above. Unused annual leave may be adjusted against the notice period with approval.
