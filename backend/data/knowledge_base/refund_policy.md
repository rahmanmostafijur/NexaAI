# Refund Policy

This policy explains how and when Nexa Commerce Ltd. refunds money for returned or cancelled orders. It should be read together with the Return Policy.

## When a Refund Is Issued

A refund is initiated within **2 business days** after a returned item passes inspection, or immediately after an order is cancelled. Customers receive an SMS and email with the refund amount and reference number.

## Refund Timelines by Payment Method

Once initiated, the time it takes for the money to reach you depends on the original payment method:

| Payment method | Time to receive the refund |
|---|---|
| bKash or Nagad | 3 to 5 business days |
| Credit or debit card | 7 to 10 business days (depends on the issuing bank) |
| Bank transfer | 5 to 7 business days |
| Cash on delivery | 5 to 7 business days, paid to a bKash, Nagad or bank account provided by the customer |

Refunds are always returned to the original payment method where possible. Cash on delivery orders cannot be refunded in cash.

## Order Cancellations

- Orders cancelled **before they are shipped** receive a full refund, including the shipping fee, within 3 business days.
- Orders cannot be cancelled after they are shipped; the customer can refuse delivery or request a return instead.
- If Nexa cancels an order because an item is out of stock, the full amount is refunded automatically.

## What Is Refunded

- The price paid for the returned items, after any discounts applied to the order.
- The original shipping fee is **not refundable**, except when the item was defective, damaged in transit, or the wrong item was delivered.
- Restocking fees (10% for opened, non-defective Electronics and Mobile Phones) and change-of-mind return shipping fees are deducted.

## Partial Refunds

A partial refund may be given when:

- Accessories, manuals or free gifts are missing from the returned item.
- The item shows signs of use or damage not caused by Nexa.
- The item is returned after the return window with special approval.

The customer is informed of the deduction and its reason before the refund is processed.

## Store Credit Option

Instead of a refund to the original payment method, customers may choose **Nexa Store Credit**. Store credit is issued instantly and includes a **5% bonus** on the refunded amount. Store credit is valid for 12 months and cannot be converted to cash.

## Refund Disputes

If a refund has not arrived within the stated timeline, contact the refunds team at refunds@nexashop.example with the refund reference number. Disputes are resolved within 7 business days.
