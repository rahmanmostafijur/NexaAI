# Return Policy

Nexa Commerce Ltd. ("Nexa") wants every customer to be satisfied with their purchase. This policy explains which items can be returned, the time limits that apply, and how to start a return. It applies to all orders placed on the Nexa website, the Nexa mobile app and Nexa retail stores.

## Standard Return Window

Most items can be returned within **30 days of delivery** for a refund or an exchange. The return window is counted from the delivery date shown in the order tracking page, not from the order date.

Gold members have an extended return window of **45 days**, and Platinum members have **60 days**, for all categories except Electronics and Mobile Phones. See the Customer Membership Policy for details.

## Electronics and Mobile Phones

Electronics and Mobile Phones have a shorter return window of **15 days from delivery**, for every membership tier.

- Unopened items in their original sealed packaging can be returned for a full refund.
- Opened items that are **not defective** can be returned within 15 days but a **10% restocking fee** is deducted from the refund.
- Items with the IMEI or serial number seal broken, or with a customer account still logged in (for example a phone with an active screen lock), cannot be accepted until the account is removed.

## Defective, Damaged or Wrong Items

If an item arrives defective, damaged in transit, or is not the item you ordered, report it **within 7 days of delivery**. Nexa will arrange a free pickup and offer either a free replacement or a full refund including the original shipping fee. Please keep the outer packaging and take photos of any visible damage, as these help us process the claim quickly.

Defects discovered after the return window are handled under the Warranty Policy.

## Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned unless they are defective or damaged on arrival:

- Groceries and perishable food items
- Opened beauty and personal care products (cosmetics, skincare, fragrances)
- Innerwear, swimwear and socks
- Gift cards and digital downloads (software keys, e-books)
- Items marked "Final Sale" or bought during clearance events
- Customised or engraved products

## Condition of Returned Items

To qualify for a return, items must be:

1. In the same condition as received, unused and undamaged.
2. Returned with all original packaging, tags, manuals, accessories and free gifts.
3. Accompanied by the invoice or order number.

Returns that do not meet these conditions may be rejected or receive a partial refund as described in the Refund Policy.

## How to Start a Return

1. Open **My Orders** in the website or app and choose **Request Return** next to the item.
2. Select the return reason and upload photos if the item is defective or damaged.
3. Inside Dhaka, a courier will collect the item within **3 business days**. Outside Dhaka, you will receive a drop-off code for the nearest partner courier point.
4. Our quality team inspects returned items within **2 business days** of receiving them and notifies you of the result by SMS and email.

## Return Shipping Costs

- Defective, damaged or wrong items: return shipping is free.
- Change-of-mind returns: the customer pays a return shipping fee of **৳100 inside Dhaka** and **৳150 outside Dhaka**, deducted from the refund.
- Gold and Platinum members get free return pickup for change-of-mind returns.

## Exchanges

Exchanges for a different size or colour of the same product are free once per order, subject to stock availability. If the replacement item costs more, the customer pays the difference; if it costs less, the difference is refunded.
