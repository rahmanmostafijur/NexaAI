# Warranty Policy

## Purpose and Scope

This Warranty Policy describes the warranty coverage Nexa Commerce Ltd. provides for products sold through the Nexa website, app and stores. Nexa honours the manufacturer's warranty on behalf of the brand and acts as the single point of contact for warranty claims, so customers do not need to contact the manufacturer directly.

The warranty period starts on the **delivery date** of the product. The warranty is valid only in Bangladesh and only for the original purchaser, and it requires the Nexa invoice or order number as proof of purchase.

A warranty covers manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. It does not replace the Return Policy: during the return window, customers may choose a return instead of a warranty repair.

## Warranty Coverage by Category

**Electronics** (laptops, televisions, audio, cameras, monitors, accessories): **12 months** warranty covering parts and labour.

**Mobile Phones**: **12 months** for the handset. Batteries, chargers and cables are covered for **6 months**.

**Home Appliances** (refrigerators, air conditioners, washing machines, microwave ovens, blenders, fans): **24 months** comprehensive warranty. Refrigerator and air conditioner **compressors** and washing machine **motors** are covered for **5 years** (parts only after the first 24 months).

**Furniture**: **6 months** against manufacturing defects such as broken joints, faulty drawer runners and frame cracks. Fabric wear, scratches and fading are not covered.

**Sports and Outdoors** equipment (fitness machines, bicycles, rackets): **3 months** warranty. Fitness machine motors are covered for 12 months.

**Toys**: a **30-day** defect warranty from delivery for electronic and battery-operated toys only.

**Fashion, Books, Beauty and Personal Care, and Groceries** carry **no warranty**; problems with these items are handled under the Return Policy.

## What the Warranty Does Not Cover

- Physical damage such as drops, cracked screens, dents and bent frames
- Liquid or moisture damage, including damage from humidity or power surges
- Repairs or modifications done by anyone other than a Nexa-authorised service centre
- Normal wear and tear, and consumable parts (filters, remote-control batteries, ink, bulbs)
- Software problems, viruses and data loss; please back up your data before handing over a device
- Products with a removed or tampered serial number
- Commercial or industrial use of products sold for household use

## How to Make a Warranty Claim

1. Open **My Orders**, choose the product and select **Warranty Claim**, or visit a Nexa service centre.
2. Nexa service centres: **Tejgaon, Dhaka** and **Agrabad, Chattogram**. Customers in other areas can request a free pickup for items under 10 kg.
3. Bring the product, all accessories and the invoice or order number.
4. Repairs are completed within **7 to 10 business days**. If a product cannot be repaired within **21 days**, Nexa replaces it with the same or an equivalent model, or issues store credit for the purchase price.
5. Replaced parts and replacement products are covered for the remaining original warranty period or 90 days, whichever is longer.

## Membership Warranty Extension

Loyalty members receive an automatic warranty extension on **Electronics, Mobile Phones and Home Appliances**:

- **Gold** members: **+3 months** added to the standard warranty.
- **Platinum** members: **+6 months** added to the standard warranty.

The extension applies to products bought while the customer held the tier.

## Nexa Extended Warranty Plan

For Electronics, Mobile Phones and Home Appliances, customers can buy the **Nexa Extended Warranty Plan** at checkout or within 30 days of delivery. It adds **12 extra months** of coverage for **8% of the product price**. The plan follows the same terms and exclusions as the standard warranty and can be transferred once to a new owner.
