# Shipping Policy

Nexa Commerce Ltd. delivers across Bangladesh and to selected international destinations. This policy describes delivery times, fees and what happens when a parcel is delayed or lost.

## Order Processing

Orders placed before **3:00 PM** on a business day are packed and handed to the courier the same day. Orders placed after 3:00 PM, or on Fridays and public holidays, are processed on the next business day.

## Domestic Delivery Times and Fees

| Destination | Delivery time | Shipping fee |
|---|---|---|
| Inside Dhaka city | 1 to 2 business days | ৳60 |
| Other divisional cities (Chattogram, Sylhet, Rajshahi, Khulna, Barishal, Rangpur, Mymensingh) | 2 to 4 business days | ৳120 |
| District towns and remote areas | 3 to 7 business days | ৳150 |

Heavy or bulky items such as furniture, refrigerators and air conditioners have a flat delivery and installation fee of **৳500** inside Dhaka and **৳900** outside Dhaka.

## Free Shipping

- Orders of **৳3,000 or more** within Bangladesh ship free (standard delivery).
- Silver members get free shipping on orders above **৳1,500**.
- Gold members get free standard shipping on all domestic orders.
- Platinum members get free **express** shipping on all domestic orders.

## Express and Same-Day Delivery

Same-day delivery is available inside Dhaka for orders placed before **12:00 PM**, for a fee of **৳150**. Express delivery to other divisional cities (next business day) costs **৳250**.

## International Shipping

Nexa ships to India, Nepal, the United Arab Emirates, Malaysia, the United Kingdom and the United States.

- Delivery time: **7 to 14 business days**.
- Shipping fees start at **৳1,500** and are calculated at checkout based on weight and destination.
- Import duties, taxes and customs fees are paid by the customer.
- Groceries, fragrances and lithium batteries sold separately cannot be shipped internationally.

## Tracking

A tracking link is sent by SMS and email once the parcel is handed to the courier. Customers can also track orders in **My Orders**.

## Delays During Festivals

During the Eid holidays and Pohela Boishakh, courier services operate with reduced capacity and deliveries may take **2 to 3 extra business days**. Order cut-off dates are announced on the website before each holiday.

## Lost or Damaged Parcels

If a parcel has not arrived **5 business days** after the latest estimated delivery date, contact support. Nexa opens an investigation with the courier; if the parcel cannot be located within 5 business days, Nexa ships a replacement or issues a full refund, including the shipping fee.

## Failed Delivery

Couriers make up to **three delivery attempts**. After three failed attempts the parcel is returned to Nexa. For prepaid orders, the amount is refunded minus the original shipping fee; cash on delivery orders are cancelled.
