# Product Guidelines

These guidelines define how products are sourced, listed, stored and handled at Nexa Commerce Ltd. They apply to the category, merchandising, warehouse and customer support teams.

## Product Listing Standards

- **Titles** follow the pattern *Brand + Model + Key attribute + Variant*, for example "Walton WFC-3F5 Refrigerator 380L Silver". Maximum 120 characters, no promotional words such as "best" or "cheapest".
- **Images:** at least **4 images** per product, minimum **1000 × 1000 pixels**, white background for the main image, no watermarks.
- **Specifications:** every listing must include dimensions, weight, warranty period and country of origin. Electronics must also list power rating and voltage (220–240 V for Bangladesh).
- **Bengali content:** every listing needs a Bengali title and a short Bengali description so customers can read product details in Bangla.

## Pricing Rules

- Discounts above **40%** of the list price require approval from the category head.
- Prices must never go below the minimum advertised price agreed with the brand.
- The list price shown as "was" must have been the actual selling price for at least 14 days in the last 90 days.

## Quality Checks on Receipt

Every inbound shipment is checked by the warehouse quality team:

1. 100% inspection for Mobile Phones and laptops (IMEI or serial number is scanned and recorded).
2. Random inspection of at least **10%** of units for all other categories.
3. Items with damaged packaging are quarantined and returned to the supplier within 7 days.

## Storage and Handling

- Electronics and Mobile Phones are stored in the climate-controlled zone at **20–25 °C** with humidity below **60%**.
- Groceries follow first-expiry-first-out; items with less than **30% of shelf life** remaining are removed from sale.
- Beauty products must be stored away from direct sunlight.
- Lithium batteries and power banks are stored in the fire-safe cage and cannot be shipped by air.

## Restocking

Stock is replenished when quantity on hand falls below the product's **reorder level**. Fast-moving products (more than 50 units sold per month) are reviewed weekly; other products are reviewed monthly.

## Restricted and Prohibited Products

Nexa does not sell weapons, prescription medicines, tobacco, alcohol, counterfeit goods, or any product without a valid import licence. Products with safety recalls are removed from sale within 24 hours of the recall notice.

## Customer Care Tips (for support agents)

- **Phone batteries:** advise customers to use the original charger and to keep the charge between 20% and 80% for longer battery life.
- **Refrigerators:** wait **3 hours** after installation before switching on so the compressor oil settles.
- **Air conditioners:** clean the filter every 2 weeks during summer.
- **Furniture:** avoid direct sunlight and use a dry cloth for cleaning.
